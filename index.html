What this PO shows

Buying group needs its code removed

The PO shows "534 - Miss Selfridge WB". The engine drops "534 - " before matching, so Miss Selfridge WB is found on the clothing list and Category becomes Clothing.

Brand is abbreviated

The PO says "MISS SELF". The engine maps it to the drop-down value Miss Selfridge, so the TRF and BV's KPI data use one spelling.

Two colours

Reporting Colour is YELLOW, Website Colour is buttermilk. Our sheet uses Reporting Colour; the prototype shows "YELLOW (buttermilk)" so the lab can match the physical sample.

One TRF per option, not per size

The PO lists four SKUs (UK 4 to 10) under option 157382210. The TRF is raised once per option and fabric.

No composition on the PO

Fibre content and weight are not on the PO, so they come from the Material Library. Our sheet already suggests adding composition to the PO.

Supplier vs factory

Supplier is Indochine International Hong Kong; the factory is Enra (F106375). Both come from the PO, so neither is typed.
